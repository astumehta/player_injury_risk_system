# Club High-Performance Load-Management Protocol
**Document Version:** 2026.4  
**Classification:** Official Club Medical & Performance Protocol  
**Compliance Requirement:** Mandatory Daily Adherence  

---

## 1. Scope and Objective
This protocol establishes explicit clinical and sports science criteria for training load prescription, injury prevention, and squad availability. All recommendations generated by automated assistive systems must strictly cite clauses from this protocol. No automated recommendation may clear a player without medical team review.

---

## 2. Workload Thresholds and ACWR Bands
- **Clause 2.1 (Optimal Sweet Spot):** Players with an Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR) between **0.80 and 1.30** are considered in the optimal conditioning zone and eligible for full training volume, provided wellness thresholds are met.
- **Clause 2.2 (Cautionary Zone):** Players with an ACWR between **1.31 and 1.50** exhibit elevated soft-tissue vulnerability. Training volume must be capped at 80% of normal match-simulation intensity; sprint distances >25 km/h must be limited to <150 meters per session.
- **Clause 2.3 (Critical Load Spike / Red Flag):** Any player exhibiting an ACWR **> 1.50** is at severe risk of acute soft-tissue injury (especially hamstring, groin, and calf complexes). The player is flagged as **HIGH RISK**. High-speed running and high-impact decelerations are prohibited.

---

## 3. Subjective Wellness and Sleep Red Flags
- **Clause 3.1 (Severe Sleep Deprivation):** Daily recorded sleep duration **< 6.0 hours** or a 3-week cumulative sleep debt **> 12 hours** impairs muscle glycogen restoration and neural reaction times. The player must be withheld from high-intensity plyometric and live contact drills.
- **Clause 3.2 (Elevated Muscular Soreness):** A muscle soreness rating **≥ 7.0 / 10** indicates incomplete structural recovery. The player must be assigned to hydrotherapy, non-weight-bearing cardiovascular conditioning (wattbike/pool), and soft-tissue release.
- **Clause 3.3 (Compound Risk Threshold):** If ACWR is **> 1.40** AND either soreness is **≥ 6.0** OR sleep is **< 6.5 hours**, the compound risk triggers mandatory squad modification (maximum 50% match load equivalent).
- **Clause 3.4 (Rapid Progression Exemption):** Any athlete returning from soft-tissue rehabilitation who demonstrates **3 consecutive sessions of muscle soreness ≤ 2.0 / 10** and **overall wellness score ≥ 8.5 / 10** is eligible for accelerated progression to Stage 4 (Full Team Contact) irrespective of acute weekly workload increments.

---

## 4. Return-to-Play (RTP) Graduated Protocols
- **Stage 1 (Clinical & Low-Impact):** Isolated gym rehabilitation, stationary bike, manual therapy. No on-pitch running.
- **Stage 2 (Linear Aerobic Running):** Non-contact running up to 60% maximum velocity. Controlled pitch-based change-of-direction.
- **Stage 3 (Position-Specific Drills):** Modified team drills, technical ball work, strictly non-contact. Total distance capped at 5 km.
- **Stage 4 (Full Team Contact & High Speed):** Participation in live tactical match play, tackling, and sprint thresholds.
- **Stage 5 (Unrestricted Match Availability):** Full clearance for selection in competitive fixture.

---

## 5. Acute Load Ceiling and Conflict Rules
- **Clause 5.1 (Weekly Minutes Cap):** A player who played **> 180 match minutes** within a 7-day rolling window must receive at least 48 hours of off-feet active recovery.
- **Clause 5.2 (Absolute Load Ceiling Rule):** Any player whose ACWR exceeds **1.55**, or whose 3-week load trend exhibits a **> 25% steep acceleration**, is strictly PROHIBITED from Stage 4 or Stage 5 full-contact activities, regardless of subjective wellness or rapid progression exemptions.
*(Note: Where Clause 3.4 and Clause 5.2 conflict for an edge-case athlete, Clause 6.1 applies).*

---

## 6. Escalation, Conflict Resolution, and Medical Sign-off
- **Clause 6.1 (Borderline / Conflicting Indicators):** In the event of conflicting protocol rules (such as exceptional wellness coexisting with an acute load spike), the system MUST declare a **PROTOCOL CONFLICT** and withhold automatic clearance. The decision escalates directly to the Medical Director.
- **Clause 6.2 (Mandatory Human Sign-Off Gate):** No automated risk alert or briefing reaches coaching staff until signed off by the Medical Director or Senior Physiotherapist. Any override of an automated High Risk flag requires a documented clinical justification on record.
- **Clause 6.3 (Protocol Gap Fallback):** If an athlete's clinical presentation or data pattern is not explicitly covered by the clauses above, the automated assistant must state: *"Protocol does not cover this edge case — immediate consultation with the club Medical Director is required."*
